Workflow

How to make better AI video prompts

  1. Describe a single shot. Video models do better with one clear action than a whole script.
  2. Control motion. Say how the camera moves and how the subject moves. Keep both simple when testing.
  3. Lock visual details. Mention identity, clothing, product shape, color, lighting and what must stay consistent.
  4. Iterate from stills. Generate a still in the image generator, then use it as an image-to-video source.
A strong video prompt reads like a tiny shot brief: subject, action, camera, lighting, continuity and what to avoid.
