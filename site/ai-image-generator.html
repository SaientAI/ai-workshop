Workflow

How to generate images in Saient

  1. Open Image Gen. Choose a base image model or download one into the app.
  2. Write a prompt. Describe subject, style, lighting, composition and details. Use the prompt maker guide if you want a template.
  3. Tune settings. Adjust size, steps, guidance, scheduler and seed when you need repeatable output.
  4. Generate locally. Keep the output on your machine and iterate without uploading private prompts or reference ideas.
